## Conversion Formulas

These formulas convert physical scales to equivalent frequencies:

1. **Timescale → frequency:** `f = 1/T` (period T in seconds → frequency in Hz)
2. **Energy → frequency (Planck-Einstein):** `f = E/h` (energy E in joules → frequency in Hz)
3. **Mass → Compton frequency:** `f = mc²/h` (mass m in kg → equivalent phase evolution frequency)
4. **Yukawa range → equivalent frequency:** `f_eq ≈ c/(2πλ)` (range λ in meters → equivalent frequency)

**Note:** The Yukawa conversion (`f_eq`) is a convenient mapping for short-range forces. It is **not** a literal oscillation in the lab apparatus, but rather a tag for the mediator's energy scale.

## Frequency Ladder (Smallest → Largest)

### 0 Hz (DC / Static)

**0 Hz (DC/static):** Steady fields and equilibria (static gravity, electrostatics, steady currents, constant stresses). These affect reality continuously without oscillation.

### Cosmic Timescales

**~2×10⁻¹⁸ Hz (Hubble rate scale):** Cosmic expansion timescale. Using H₀ ≈ 67.4 km/s/Mpc, this is approximately 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹. This is the regime where cosmological constraints test MQGT-SCF.

**~10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁷ Hz (nanohertz gravitational waves):** Gravitational waves with periods of years to decades, targeted by Pulsar Timing Arrays (PTA band).

**3.17×10⁻⁸ Hz (1/year):** Annual cycles (Earth orbit, seasonal forcing).

**1.16×10⁻⁵ Hz (1/day):** Daily cycles (Earth rotation, circadian entrainment).

### Gravitational Wave Bands

**~10⁻⁴ to 1 Hz (LISA band):** Space-based gravitational-wave band (millihertz to hertz).

**~20 Hz to 5 kHz (LIGO band):** Ground-based interferometer sensitivity band.

### Life and Mechanics

**~0.1 to 10 Hz:** Human-scale oscillators (respiration, locomotion rhythms, structural sway).

**~0.5 to 100 Hz:** Brain/EEG bands (delta: 0.5–4 Hz, theta: 4–8 Hz, alpha: 8–13 Hz, beta: 13–30 Hz, gamma: 30–100 Hz). These are real physiological oscillations relevant to experienced reality, not universal constants.

**7.83 Hz (Schumann resonance fundamental):** Earth-ionosphere cavity resonance (plus harmonics ~14–34 Hz). Real geophysics, often over-mythologized.

### Sound and Human Perception

**20 Hz to 20 kHz:** Typical human hearing band.

**~10⁶ to 10⁹ Hz (MHz to GHz):** Ultrasound and radio engineering (imaging, communications, electronics).

### Electromagnetic Spectrum

**1.420×10⁹ Hz (hydrogen 21-cm line):** Neutral hydrogen hyperfine transition (radio astronomy).

**9,192,631,770 Hz (Cs-133 hyperfine transition):** Defines the SI second. This is frequency literally defining reality's measurement grid.

**~1.6×10¹¹ Hz (~160 GHz):** Cosmic microwave background peak (CMB blackbody at 2.725 K).

**~10¹³ Hz:** Infrared (thermal radiation, molecular vibrations).

**4.3×10¹⁴ to 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz:** Visible light (red to blue).

**~10¹⁵ to 10¹⁷ Hz:** UV to X-rays (ionization, atomic processes).

**~10¹⁹ to 10²³ Hz:** Gamma rays (nuclear/particle processes).

### Quantum Energy-as-Frequency Scales

**1 eV ↔ 2.418×10¹⁴ Hz:** Useful conversion (visible photons are a few eV).

**Electron rest energy ↔ ~1.24×10²⁰ Hz:** Electron Compton frequency (mₑc²/h).

**Proton rest energy ↔ ~2.27×10²³ Hz:** Proton Compton frequency (mₚc²/h).

**Higgs mass scale (~125 GeV) ↔ ~3×10²⁵ Hz:** Higgs energy-as-frequency via E/h.

### Quantum Gravity Scale

**~1.85×10⁴³ Hz (Planck frequency):** Via Planck time (tₚ ≈ 5.391×10⁻⁴⁴ s → fₚ ≈ 1/tₚ). Standard "quantum gravity yardstick" where current physics stops being trustworthy.
