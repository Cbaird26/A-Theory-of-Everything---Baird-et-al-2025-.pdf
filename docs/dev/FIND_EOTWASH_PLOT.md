# How to Find and Download the Eöt-Wash Plot Image

**Quick guide to get the figure you need for digitization**

---

## Option 1: Direct Paper Access (Recommended)

### Step 1: Access the Paper

The Eöt-Wash group's PRL 2016 paper is:
- **Title:** "Tests of the Gravitational Inverse-Square Law below the Dark-Energy Length Scale"
- **Authors:** Wagner et al. (Eöt-Wash Group)
- **PRL 116, 131102 (2016)**

**Access via:**
1. **APS Physical Review Letters:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.131102
2. **arXiv:** Search for "Eöt-Wash PRL 2016 fifth force"
3. **Eöt-Wash Group Website:** https://npl.washington.edu/eotwash

### Step 2: Find the Figure

Look for a figure showing:
- **X-axis:** λ (range) in meters (log scale, typically 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻² m)
- **Y-axis:** α (strength) dimensionless (log scale, typically 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻²)
- **Title:** Usually something like "Constraints on Yukawa-type deviations" or "Fifth-force limits"

### Step 3: Save the Figure

**If PDF:**
- Open the PDF
- Navigate to the figure
- Take a screenshot:
  - **Mac:** Cmd + Shift + 4, drag to select just the plot area
  - **Windows:** Snipping Tool, select the plot area
- Save as: `eotwash_plot.png` in your Downloads folder

**If high-res image available:**
- Right-click the figure → Save Image As
- Save as: `eotwash_plot.png` in Downloads

## Option 2: AxionLimits Website (Alternative)

1. Go to: https://cajohare.github.io/AxionLimits/
2. Navigate to "Fifth Force" or "Equivalence Principle" constraints
3. Look for Eöt-Wash limits on the plot
4. If available, click "Download (.png)" or take a screenshot

---

## Option 3: Use Existing Plot from Literature

If you have access to any review paper or compilation that includes Eöt-Wash limits:
- The plot should show α vs λ (Yukawa constraints)
- Log-log scale
- Eöt-Wash typically appears as a curve in the mm-cm range

---

## What the Plot Should Look Like

**Key features:**
- Log-log axes
- X-axis: λ (meters) from ~10⁻⁴ to ~10⁻² (mm to cm range)
- Y-axis: α (dimensionless) from ~10⁻⁶ to ~10⁻²
- A curve showing the exclusion limit (points above/beyond the curve are ruled out)
- May have multiple curves (different experiments), but you want the Eöt-Wash one
